Scheduled Downtime & Auto-Shutdown

During the daily downtime window, any running wiki build is gracefully paused and the local llama-servers are shut down to free memory. At the end of the window the servers come back up and the wiki build resumes.

When on, clicking Stop on a wiki build also frees the ~7-10 GB of memory the llama-servers use. They stay paused until you next click in Chat, Documents, or Wiki — or click the status pill to start them manually.
